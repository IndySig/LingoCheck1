About LingoCheck

Your AI translation, checked by a human expert.

Why we started

The translation industry is changing fast. More and more organisations use AI to translate their content, and that makes sense: it's quick and cheap. But AI translations still contain mistakes. Sentences go missing, content gets invented, terminology slips, and the result often sounds just slightly off. At the same time, we saw translators and translation agencies losing work, even though their expertise is needed more than ever.

We thought there had to be a better way.

What we do

LingoCheck checks your AI translations. You keep using the AI tool you already have. We make sure the result is right before it reaches your readers.

Every translation is reviewed by a professional human revisor, not just another algorithm. They check that nothing is missing or added, that the text is correct and natural, and that your terminology is used consistently. Our platform helps revisors work quickly, so you get expert quality without the time or cost of a full traditional revision.

Built with translation experts

We developed LingoCheck together with Interlingo, an experienced translation agency. Their knowledge of what goes wrong in translations, and of how professionals catch it, is built into how LingoCheck works.

For revisors

AI isn't going away, and neither is the need for human judgement. LingoCheck gives professional translators the chance to put their expertise to wider use and take on more work.

Where we're going

Our goal is to become the standard for quality-checking AI translations, so that using AI never means compromising on quality.
